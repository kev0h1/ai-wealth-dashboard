# Mirror trait clarity · G332

Mode: Operate.

This is a preview-only design round for the choice area of Mirror trait cards. It keeps the Calm Cockpit's calm, factual reading: observations lead; a person chooses whether Sorted should coach on that observation; no coaching begins before a change choice.

Both directions use one reading grid regardless of whether a category mark exists. Evidence therefore begins at the same left edge on every card. The rule appears once, above the list: each trait is chosen separately, and keeping it means no coaching.

- A, **Two clear answers**, makes both durable choices visible in a compact equal pair.
- B, **One decision row**, starts with a single "Choose how to use this" control and reveals the two mutually exclusive answers only when needed. It gives an unchosen card a quieter ending without hiding the choice.

The examples are synthetic and local. They never call the live API or alter saved choices. Query controls: `?variant=a|b&state=all|kept|change|pending|save-error|hidden|long&mode=light|dark`.
