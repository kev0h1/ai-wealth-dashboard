# G453 correction scope

Kevin chose B, the inline review, on 10 November 2026. The production teaching sheet shows the selected classification and payment counts before confirmation. Savings and investments reach review directly. Goal and offline account resolutions retain their real destinations, and card repayments retain their dedicated flag. The comparison previews are retired.

This payment only is the default. The second option covers eligible booked payments on or before the selected date. The future option covers the booked history and saves a rule for new matching payments. All saved custom categories remain protected, including the selected payment. An already categorised payment can still provide the identity for a wider correction to eligible matches.

The server owns eligibility, counts and updates. Matching uses the current booked transaction source, account owner, payee identity, direction and currency. Legacy rows can use the canonical payee key derived from their stored fields. A payment without a usable payee identity supports a one-off correction. Undo restores only fields still owned by that operation. The first two options do not create a future rule or contribute to merchant and card-repayment learning. Future rules retain their typed classification and can be removed from the saved receipt or Settings → Category rules.

Changes invalidate the same derived figures as other corrections. The transfer review batches its refresh through H145. Outside that review, figures refresh after the save, with a retry that only refreshes figures. The server keeps a receipt for retry and Undo for seven days. Saved categories and active future rules are stored separately.
