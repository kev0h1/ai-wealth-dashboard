# G287: approved state-led cash forecasts

Kevin approved the refined E design on 21 October 2026. This production fold-in supersedes G218’s hero figure tints and the earlier A/B/C/D/F proposals.

- Home and Upcoming use 30px ink figures, 16px corners, a compact right-aligned status and a 20px header-row-to-figure gap. Confirmed payment shortfalls say Short on both cards. Negative cash and reserve-adjusted shortfalls keep their actual minus signs.
- Red marks a genuine dated cash gap, including an earlier gap before later income. Plans gap, Below buffer, Tight, No headroom and Check card bill are amber. On track is green. Status text and icons carry the colour; neither main money figure does.
- Home remains the dated cash low point less buffer and reserves. Upcoming remains the balance at payday. Card check keeps £0 available and its separate signed safety calculation. Existing calculations, cash-transfer exclusions, card activity, spend-from guidance, caveats and conditional payment/Cards and Accounts actions remain.
- Upcoming derives risk from its existing dated pooled walk, strictly before payday and only with a known spendable balance. Savings fallback and stale balances cannot establish safety. A nonblocking Left to spend read provides buffer, card and Tight context only when the forecast periods and complete evidence agree. Every cashflow replacement invalidates that adjunct immediately; out-of-order responses are discarded. Pending context leaves a reserved, inaccessible blank status slot until the read settles, preventing a flash of a default icon or verdict. A reserved minimum status width keeps the title and figure steady on narrow phones. Missing or conflicting evidence after settlement is neutral Check forecast, never invented reassurance. Calendar-month mode does not borrow payday-specific caution metadata.
- Penny receives the same signed Home figure and the actual Upcoming status, including Earlier gap for a positive endpoint after an earlier shortfall. Existing balance masking remains in force.
- The temporary design round is retired under H113. Its realistic fixture data and production regression checks move to frontend/scripts; no production code imports a design route and no preview adapter ships.
