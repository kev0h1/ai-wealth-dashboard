# Plus tier: proposed Terms wording (B53, for Kevin's approval)

Status: proposal only. TERMS.md and the generated PDFs are not changed by B53.

## What TERMS.md says today

TERMS.md names no tier, price or limit. Section 9 ("Fees and subscriptions") speaks of "free and paid subscription tiers" generally, section 8's connector paragraph says connector use "counts against your plan's monthly allowance and is available on the plans that include it", and the plan names live only in the app. So the Lite, Standard and Plus change needs no edit to keep the Terms accurate.

## Optional additions (each needs your approval, and a PDF regeneration if accepted)

1. Plan limits. Add to Section 9 after the first paragraph: "Each paid plan has a limit on the number of bank accounts you can connect and on the number of Penny messages you can send each month. The limits for each plan are shown to you before you purchase and in Settings, Your plan. Every paid plan includes every feature."
2. Fair use on unlimited accounts. The Plus plan has no fixed account limit. Unit economics (docs/pricing/tiering-unit-economics-mcp-2026-09.md, section 4a) show Plus stops paying for itself above roughly 46 connected accounts at the full Penny allowance, so add: "The Plus plan allows an unlimited number of connected accounts for personal use. We may contact you if your use is far beyond normal personal use, for example connecting accounts you do not own."
3. Plan changes for existing subscribers. Add to "Changing your plan": "If we retire or rename a plan, we will move you to the nearest equivalent plan at the same renewal date and the same or a lower price, and tell you before your next renewal. You can cancel at any time."  This covers existing Max and Connect subscribers moving to Plus. Note: Plus monthly is £12.99, below the old Max £16.99 and equal to the old Connect price, so no existing subscriber pays more. Max subscribers' Penny allowance falls from 400 to 300 messages a month, which this clause should not hide; tell affected users in the notice.

## Decision needed

Whether to adopt any of the three. Without them the Terms remain accurate but silent on limits and on retired plans.
