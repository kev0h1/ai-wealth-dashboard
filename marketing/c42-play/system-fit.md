## Handoff

C42 is an ordinary extension of the approved C22 campaign. It preserves
PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md and does not establish a new visual world. The
specialist Impeccable documenter role was not exposed by this harness, so a
fresh standard independent agent supplied this documentation. The context

records the intended fit and review boundaries. It

## Asset and compliance boundaries

- The film is 1920 x 1080, 30 seconds, and uses the original C22
  instrumental motifs. Captions describe the on-screen fictional examples;
  there is no spoken narration.
- The three promotional visuals are the launch template, Penny charts, and
  payday plans. They are sourced from production chart rendering and contain
  no added text, wordmark, outer frame, or rounded external corner.
- The two ZIPs are theme-specific and contain the MP4, poster, VTT, three
  PNGs, copy, and manifest. C42 writes to its own media directory and does not
  overwrite C22 assets.
- Source hashes, PNG `Source` and `impeccable:prompt` metadata, manifest
  records, and the fixture boundary establish provenance. No external API
  requests are expected.
- The pack is creative-review material. Play Console eligibility, final copy,
  event dates and audience, YouTube privacy and embed settings, native parity,
  upload, and publication remain outside this documenter's authority.
