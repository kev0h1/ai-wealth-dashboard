# C35 homepage design round

Thesis: make one honest, estimated everyday-money view useful before payday, without promising outcomes.

- A, Product first: a calm asymmetric product proof with a sequence of practical features.
- B, Everyday money: an editorial real-life moment leading into grounded product evidence.
- C, Clear perspective: a broad question-led stage where the estimate and product proof arrive together.

The design uses Sorted's slate canvas, indigo action colour, self-hosted Figtree and JetBrains Mono, 16px surfaces and 12px controls. Dials are variance 5, motion 2 and density 4. Penny's gradient is not used. The site is static, responsive, reduced-motion safe and has no data collection UI.

This package is preview-only. Prices and disclosures are checked against product sources; real store links remain unknown. After Kevin chooses a direction, build the full public-site information architecture, then establish canonical metadata, sitemap and Vercel settings once its domain is known. Asset provenance remains in the root-owned `asset-provenance.json` and `editorial-morning.prompt.txt`.
